## 1. Test-Point Architecture & Physical PCB Placement Guidelines

Eighteen dedicated through-hole test points (**`TP1` through `TP18`**, footprint `TestPoint_THTPad_D1.5mm_Drill0.9mm` / 2.54 mm machined test turret) are distributed across the 8 functional schematic blocks to allow 100% non-invasive DMM and oscilloscope verification of every power rail, LC Pi-filter node, programmable DAC source, Kelvin DUT terminal, `ADS1115` sense input, hardware failsafe interrupt, opto-isolated UART channel, and the single-point `AGND`/`DGND` star-ground bridge (`R_STAR`).

- **Reference Ground Selection Rule:**
  - When probing **Digital / Charger / UI Test Points (`TP1`, `TP2`, `TP3`, `TP17`)**, clip the DMM / oscilloscope ground lead to the **`DGND`** side of `R_STAR` (`TP18_DGND` or `J_CYD_UART` Pin 2).
  - When probing **Analog Core / AFE / Metrology Test Points (`TP4`–`TP16`, `TP18_AGND`)**, clip the DMM / oscilloscope ground lead exclusively to the **`AGND`** side of `R_STAR` (`TP18_AGND` or `J_KELVIN` Pin 4) so digital return currents do not corrupt microvolt ripple measurements.

## 3. Step-by-Step First-Power-On Bench Verification Procedure Using `TP1`–`TP18`

1. **Pre-Insert Power & Boost Trim Check (All Socketed ICs & MCUs Removed, `JP_M1_3V3` & `JP_M2_3V3` Open):**
   - Set `SW_PWR = OFF`. Connect a $3.7\text{ V}$ Non-HV LiPo battery ($3.80\text{ V}$ nominal storage charge) to `J_BAT`. Verify current into `J_BAT+` is **$0.000\text{ }\mu\text{A}$** and **`TP2` (`V_BUS_SYS`) = $0.000\text{ V}$**.
   - Plug in USB-C ($5.0\text{ V}$) with `SW_PWR = OFF`. Verify **`TP1` (`V_USB_PROT`) = $5.00\text{ V}$**, **`TP2` (`V_BUS_SYS`) = $0.000\text{ V}$**, and battery charge current is **$0.000\text{ mA}$** (confirming zero blind charging at $4.208\text{ V}$ default!).
   - Throw `SW_PWR = ON`. Verify **`TP2` (`V_BUS_SYS`) $\in [3.50\text{ V}, 4.20\text{ V}]$** and **`TP3` (`+3V3_DIG`) = $3.300\text{ V} \pm 0.016\text{ V}$**, with Top-Bezel Green `LED_PWR` illuminated.
   - Temporarily jumper `ANA_PWR_EN` (`U11` Socket Pin 8) to `+3V3_DIG` (`U11` Socket Pin 9) via a $10\text{ k}\Omega$ test resistor to turn ON `Q_ANA_N` and `Q_ANA_SW`. Verify **`TP4` (`V_ANA_IN`) $\approx V_{\text{BUS\_SYS}}$**.
   - Adjust the multi-turn trimpot on `MOD_BOOST` (`MT3608`) until **`TP5` (`V_14V_FILT`) reads $14.44\text{ V} \pm 0.10\text{ V}$** ($14.50\text{ V}$ at `V_BOOST_RAW`).
   - Verify **`TP6` (`+12V_A`) = $12.00\text{ V} \pm 0.20\text{ V}$**, **`TP7` (`+9V_REF`) = $9.00\text{ V} \pm 0.15\text{ V}$**, **`TP8` (`+9V_LIM`) = $8.92\text{ V} \pm 0.15\text{ V}$**, **`TP9` (`+3V3_ANA`) = $3.300\text{ V} \pm 0.016\text{ V}$**, and **`TP10` (`+3V3_ADC`) = $3.299\text{ V} \pm 0.016\text{ V}$**.
2. **15 mA Active Limiter & `FAULT_OVC_N` Hardware Trip Test:**
   - With the analog rails still energized and no ICs seated, connect a $100\,\Omega$ $0.5\text{ W}$ test resistor from **`TP8` (`+9V_LIM`)** to **`TP18_AGND` (`AGND`)** in series with a DMM ammeter.
   - Verify that current is strictly clamped to **$15.12\text{ mA} \pm 1.50\text{ mA}$** and that **`TP16` (`FAULT_OVC_N`)** immediately transitions from **$+3.30\text{ V}$ (`HIGH`) to $\le 0.25\text{ V}$ (`LOW`)**. Remove the test short and temporary `ANA_PWR_EN` jumper.
3. **Standby Cutoff & Opto-Isolation Zero Back-Powering Verification:**
   - Power OFF (`SW_PWR = OFF`), insert all socketed DIP ICs (`U1..U11`, `U_ISO1`, `U_ISO2`), modules (`MOD_ADS`, `MOD_TFT1`, `MOD_TFT2`, `U_MCU1`, `U_MCU2`), and install jumpers `JP_M1_3V3` and `JP_M2_3V3`.
   - Set **`SW_STBY = STANDBY` (`LOW`)** and throw **`SW_PWR = ON`**.
   - Verify **`TP3` (`+3V3_DIG`) = $3.300\text{ V}$**, Cyan `LED_STBY_RF` is ON, and **`TP4` (`V_ANA_IN`) = `TP6` (`+12V_A`) = `TP8` (`+9V_LIM`) = `TP9` (`+3V3_ANA`) = `TP10` (`+3V3_ADC`) = $0.000\text{ V}$ ($\le 0.5\text{ mV}$)**, confirming $0.000\text{ mA}$ Analog Core drain and $0.000\text{ }\mu\text{A}$ UART back-powering through `U_ISO1`/`U_ISO2`.
